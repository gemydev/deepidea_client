DeepIdea (“DeepIdea”, “we”, “us”) operates the DeepIdea mobile application (the “App”). This policy explains what personal data the App collects, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the rights you have over it.

By creating an account or using the App, you agree to this policy.

1.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device and technical data — device model, operating system version, app version, language, time zone, and a randomly generated device/installation identifier.
  • Network state — whether the device is online and the connection type, so the App can queue work while offline.
  • Usage and analytics events — screens opened and actions taken inside the App, collected through Google Firebase Analytics.
  • Diagnostic data — crash and error information used to fix defects.

1.4 Payment information

Payments are processed by our payment provider EdfaPay. Card numbers, CVV codes, and other full payment credentials are entered into the provider’s secure page or SDK and are not collected, stored, or accessible by DeepIdea. We receive only the transaction result — such as amount, currency, status, date, and a masked card reference — and store it with your invoice.

1.5 Data we do not collect

We do not collect precise device location, contacts, call logs, SMS messages, or health data.

2. How we use your information

We use personal data to:

  1. Create, secure, and administer your account;
  2. Provide the App’s core features — services, orders, invoices, contracts, chat, and voice/video calls;
  3. Process payments and issue invoices and loyalty points;
  4. Send transactional notifications (order status, incoming calls, new messages, security alerts);
  5. Provide customer support and respond to your requests;
  6. Analyse aggregate usage so we can improve stability and design;
  7. Detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, abuse, and security incidents;
  8. Meet our legal, tax, and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your chat content or uploaded files for advertising.

4. Sharing your information

We share personal data only with:

  • Service providers acting on our instructions:
    • ZEGOCLOUD — real-time voice and video call infrastructure;
    • Google Firebase (Google LLC) — analytics and app infrastructure;
    • EdfaPay — payment processing;
    • Our hosting and backend providers, which operate the App’s servers and store your account data.
  • Professional advisers (accountants, auditors, lawyers) under confidentiality obligations.
  • Authorities, where we are legally required to disclose data, or to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.
  • A successor entity, in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets — subject to this policy.

Each provider receives only the data needed for its function and is bound by contract to protect it.

5. International transfers

Our providers may process data on servers outside your country, including outside the EEA. Where required, such transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. Security

We protect your data with encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), access controls limiting staff access to what their role requires, hashed password storage, and optional biometric app-lock on your device. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the competent authority where the law requires it.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you;
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data — much of it directly in the profile screen;
  • Delete your account and data;
  • Restrict or object to certain processing;
  • Port your data to another provider in a machine-readable format;
  • Withdraw consent at any time;
  • Complain to your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these, email [SUPPORT EMAIL]. We respond within 30 days. We may ask you to verify your identity before acting on a request.

You can also manage permissions (camera, microphone, notifications, Bluetooth) at any time in your device settings.

9. Children

The App is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact [SUPPORT EMAIL] and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date above always reflects the current version. For material changes we will notify you in the App or by email before the change takes effect. Continuing to use the App after that means you accept the updated policy.
